## Facts

- F2 SETTLED: ReplicatedStorage.AbilityRegistry (--!strict) returns {Powers, Nerfs}. Powers[id] = {Id, Name, Description, Cooldown, Key?, NerfPool (exactly 3), OnApply?}; Nerfs[id] = {Id, Name, Description, OnApply?}. Stat effects are deltas (Leap +30 JP, not +25 as F1 guessed; Juggernaut +50 HP and x1.15 scale). ServerScriptService.AbilityService (ModuleScript): Equip(player, character, {powers, nerfs}) applies OnApply then sets Health=MaxHealth; no OnRemove, so a stat reset needs a fresh character (LoadCharacter). Active powers (R dash, E shockwave) only fire when player attribute Fighting == true. Remote: ReplicatedStorage.Remotes.Ability. Source: script_read in Studio, 2026-11-25 22:55.

## Decisions
- D1: Web prototype used plain JS instead of React + Tailwind. Why: one small page, no build step, same result. Rejected: React/Tailwind via CDN (extra weight for no gain).
- D2: Panel is open to all players, lobby only; toggles apply to the real character through AbilityService.Equip. Why: the user's answers, 2026-11-25 22:55. Rejected: Studio-only gate; readout only.
- F3 SETTLED: Match state signals. MatchInstance sets the player attribute Fighting (true = standing, false = knocked out, nil = not in a round) and Arena. AbilityService.Equip sets the Loadout attribute, and TableManager clears it after the series. Tables publish InMatch on their model. During the map vote or draft, players sit at a table with Fighting = nil. MatchInstance calls LoadCharacter + Equip every round, so a test loadout can't leak into a match. Source: script_read of MatchInstance + TableManager, 2026-11-25 22:55.
- F4 SETTLED (re-checked 2026-11-25 22:57, AbilityService line 60 + MeleeController 181/193, MovementController 26, MoveFxRelay 28 all gate on Fighting == true): In the lobby, R/E actives won't fire, because AbilityService `standing()` needs Fighting == true. Passive stat powers (Juggernaut, Leap and the stat nerfs) do work. Options: a TestMode attribute that standing() also accepts (lobby only), or show the actives as "match only" in the panel. Not decided.
